History

Ninjasus

What would now be called Eleuedla was originally called the Republic of Ninjago, after the popular T.V. series of the same name. This state, if it could be called such, was a constitutional republic, alike to that of the United States, and its constitution was found and vaguely edited by the Emperor (which functioned as the President) to say Ninjago instead of the placeholder name. The original Congress consisted of the Emperor's parents, who did very little during their short term, much to the annoyance of the Emperor who abolished the government.

Following this, the first Constitution was taped to the wall of the Emperor's bedroom.
