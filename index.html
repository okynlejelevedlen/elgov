Micronationalism

While you're waiting, you may want to learn about micronationalism! The hobby is based on the Montevideo Convention on the Rights and Duties of States. A State is defined by Article 1 of the aforementioned convention, and must have (a) a permanent population; (b) a defined territory ; (c) government ; and (d) capacity to enter into relations with the other States (UN, p. 25). Additionally, the document states that the political existence of the State is independent of recognition by the other States (ibid.).

About Eleuedla

Additionally, it is important to mention some details of the Eleued nation-state. Firstly, we are a Catholic State, and the view of the Government agrees with the position of the Papacy in regards to Church and State. Secondly, it is in part an attempt to create a unique culture in the ways of language and other aspects. Our view of nationality is not a simple 'legal' proposition, but requires alsosome form of ethnic and cultural assimilation. The only exception to this is for the first generation of this Nation.

The Kingdom of Eleuedla currently maintains relations with the Second Kingdom of Suteria as well as the Sprinske Empire. There are, additionally, two states within Eleuedla, those being the Archduchy of Saint Augustine and the Duchy of Sejgon, respectively. The particulars of both states are detailed in History.
