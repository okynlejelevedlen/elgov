Provisional Act on Diplomacy

ez Les Provisional de Diplomatgia

In order to keep Clovisian isolation and in order to protect it from malicious outsiders, this act seeks to place restrictions on which foreigners to do business with, in order to protect the national values of unity, traditionalism, and submission to God and His Church.

Restrictions regarding Ideology -
The State of Clovisia shall not seek to do diplomacy or business with communist or racialist states, nor with states that require idolization of the state. These are against the faith and must be rejected. States that require idolization of greed or of money must also be avoided.

Restrictions regarding Culture -
The State if Clovisia will not make any agreements with any nations that is not Christian. If the state petitioning to Clovisia permits evils condemned by the Catholic Church, is primarily of heathen, or does not permit or allow the rights of the Catholic Church, Clovisia shall not engage in diplomacy. If the state in question does not respect or permit the expression of the Eleued culture, Clovisia shall not engage in diplomacy.

Other Provisions -
Foreign states or Clovisia may be the first to communicate through the chosen diplomatic authority. If the foreign state is initiating, Clovisia must be given a maximum of three weeks for it to investigate the initiating nation. If the Clovisian diplomatic authority rejects the proposal, it will inform the initiating nation.
Amendment to this document will be allowed every one year.
